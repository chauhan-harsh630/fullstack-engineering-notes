# 🌐 What is the Internet?

## 📖 Overview

The **Internet** is a global network of billions of interconnected computers and electronic devices. It relies on standard protocols (such as TCP/IP) to route packets of digital data across physical infrastructure including fiber optic cables, satellite links, and cellular networks.

> **Key Distinction**: The **Internet** is the underlying physical and networking infrastructure. The **World Wide Web (WWW)** is an application service built *on top* of the Internet that allows users to access web pages via HTTP.

---

## 🏗️ Physical Infrastructure Components

* **ISPs (Internet Service Providers)**: Companies (e.g., Comcast, AT&T, Jio) that connect individual local devices to regional networks.
* **Subsea Cables**: Transoceanic fiber-optic cables that carry over 99% of international data traffic.
* **Routers & Switches**: Specialized networking hardware that inspects data packet headers and routes them to their destination IP addresses.

---

## 🧠 Interview Questions

### What is the difference between the Internet and the World Wide Web?
The Internet is the global hardware and network connectivity infrastructure (like roads). The Web is an application system of hyperlinked documents accessed using browsers over HTTP (like cars traveling on those roads).

---

## 📌 Summary
* The Internet connects global devices via standardized TCP/IP packet networking.
